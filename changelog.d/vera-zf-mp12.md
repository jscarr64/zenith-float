- `ExactNum::dim(&self, y: &Self, p: usize, rm: RoundingMode) -> Result<ExactNum, Error>` (new, MP12, MPFR `mpfr_dim`): `x - y` rounded once at `p` bits with `rm` when `x > y`, else `+0` (always positive zero). NaN input is refused (a carried error propagates, a bare NaN is `InvalidArgument`); a genuine infinity from an infinite operand is `Ok(Inf)`; a finite difference that overflows under a rounding mode that rounds away from zero is `ExponentOverflow(Pos)`, while toward-zero modes return the largest finite value, matching `tests/overflow_modes.rs`. Tested in `zenith-float-num/tests/mp12_dim.rs` against exact integers and powers of two, signed zeros, infinities, overflow in every mode, precisions 1, 53, 63, 64, 65, 113, 128, 200, and the 1 - 2^-200 rounding row at p=64. Additive.

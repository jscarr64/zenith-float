- `RoundingMode::None` at the entry points that round the requested precision themselves (`ldexp`, `nth_root`, `cbrt`, `sqrt`, `reciprocal`, the elementary and special functions, the constants) keeps `rp(p, 64)` bits on every target, like the core operations. On 32-bit targets these cut at `rp(p, 32)` bits (a 224-bit `nth_root` at `p = 200`; `ldexp(x, 0, 1, None)` of a 64-bit `x` flagged inexact). 64-bit results are unchanged.

- `ExactNum::modf(&self, p: usize, rm: RoundingMode) -> Result<(ExactNum, ExactNum), Error>` (new, MP16, MPFR `mpfr_modf`): integer part (trunc toward 0) and fractional part with the sign of `x`, each rounded to `p` bits with `rm`, rebuilt from the mantissa words without copying `self` (Rusty REWRITE ruling 2026-11-05: no helper-copy site, no exception, no baseline raise). `+Inf` gives `(+Inf, +0)` and `-Inf` gives `(-Inf, -0)`; NaN input is refused (a carried error propagates, a bare NaN is `InvalidArgument`). Tested in `zenith-float-num/tests/mp16_modf.rs` against exact values, signed zeros, infinities, huge and tiny values, every rounding mode, precisions 1, 53, 63, 64, 65, 113, 128, 200, and the 0.5+2^-100 rounding row at p=64. Additive.

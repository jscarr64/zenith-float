- **Fix (2.0): cost estimates no longer overflow on 32-bit targets or truncate on 64-bit** (M32). calc_mul_cost formed isqrt(p)^3 in usize, which overflows a 32-bit usize from p = 2_643_876 bits (1626^3 > 2^32 - 1; a debug panic, a wrapped estimate in release), and passed p and niter through `as u32` (wrong above 2^32 on 64-bit); series_cost could overflow u64 at large p. Costs are now exact u128 integers clamped to u64::MAX. Only the choice of argument-reduction steps and series length can change, never a rounded result. Tests: zenith-float-num/src/common/util.rs, ops/cos.rs, ops/series.rs `cost_overflow_tests`.

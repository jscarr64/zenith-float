- **Fix (2.0): `constant_time_eq` no longer reports equal for slices whose lengths differ by a multiple of 256** (M34). The length mismatch was folded in as `(a.len() ^ b.len()) as u8`, which drops every bit above the low byte, so a 256-byte all-zero slice compared equal to an empty one. It is now `u8::from(a.len() != b.len())`. Test: `zenith-float-num/tests/m34_constant_time_eq.rs::constant_time_eq_length_mismatch_survives_mod_256`.

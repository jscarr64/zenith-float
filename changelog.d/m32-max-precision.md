- **Breaking (2.0): `MAX_PRECISION` is `2^32 - 1` on every target** (was `2^60 - 1`; M32). `p > 2^32 - 1` is `InvalidArgument`. New `MAX_ALLOC_BYTES = 2^31` and `check_alloc_bytes(n, elem_bytes)`: every heap buffer is checked against the 2 GiB single-buffer budget before allocating, and an over-budget request is `MemoryAllocation` with nothing allocated, identical on every target. The 4p working width at `MAX_PRECISION` (`2^28` words) fits the budget exactly. The OS can still OOM-kill a machine with too little RAM (see `doc/LIBRARY.md` §5).

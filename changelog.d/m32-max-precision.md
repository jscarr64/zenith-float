- **Breaking (2.0): `MAX_PRECISION` is `2^30 - 2^18` (1,073,479,680 bits) on every target** (was `2^60 - 1`; M32, lowered from `2^32 - 1` in M32-R). `p > MAX_PRECISION` is `InvalidArgument`. It is the largest precision at which add, sub, mul, div, sqrt and the radix conversions are correct with every single buffer inside the budget: the multiply's FFT work buffer peaks at 1,612,234,800 bytes there (at `2^32 - 1` it needed 6,643,780,704, and `2^32 - 1` was not a word multiple, so every operation at that precision refused). It is a whole number of words, and `2 * MAX_PRECISION < 2^32` is const-asserted. New `MAX_ALLOC_BYTES = 2^31` and `check_alloc_bytes(n, elem_bytes)`: every heap buffer is checked against the 2 GiB single-buffer budget before allocating; a request of exactly `2^31` bytes is accepted, and anything larger is `MemoryAllocation` with nothing allocated, identical on every target. Families whose working buffers exceed the budget at `MAX_PRECISION` refuse with `MemoryAllocation`. The OS can still OOM-kill a machine with too little RAM.

### zenith-float MPFR comparison tests replay committed goldens; rug and gmp-mpfr-sys are gone

- `--features mpfr-tests` no longer links MPFR: every MPFR/GMP call of the 141 comparison tests replays a committed
  golden stream (`zenith-float-num/tests/mpfr/golden/`, 122 streams, 109.7 MB, binary `zfgold2`, SHA256SUMS checked),
  generated by `tools/mpfr_golden/gen.py` (python-flint, Ziv loop, pinned team env). `tests/mpfr/mp.rs` is a safe
  stand-in for the MPFR API with MPFR 4.2.2's range, subnormal and rounding rules; `tests/mpfr/exact.rs` computes the
  exact operations (add, sub, mul, div, remainder) itself, checked record for record against the generator in gen mode.
- One tier: every test runs its full case count (MPFR_TIER is gone); the 1,426,376 records are the same queries and
  answers as a full-tier text regeneration. `tools/mpfr_golden/crosscheck.py` checks them against MPFR (gmpy2): 600,295
  in gmpy2's exponent range agree, 0 differ; the published 50-digit pi, ln 2 and Euler gamma agree.
- Two real bugs found on the way are fixed in their own commits (exp bit-burst inexact flag, rem_pi below 2 pi).
- `unsafe_pending.tsv` is empty and the scanner fails on any entry.

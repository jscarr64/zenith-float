- **Fix (2.0): `ExactNum::rem` no longer overflows the exponent difference at extreme spreads.** `rem` formed `e1eff - e2eff` in `i64`, which overflows when the dividend is near `EXPONENT_MAX` and the divisor near `EXPONENT_MIN` (a debug panic at `num.rs:670`, a wrong nonzero remainder in release). The gap is now computed wide (`i128`) and `2^gap` is reduced modulo the divisor without ever materialising the gap as a narrower integer; gaps above `usize::MAX` use the new `pow2_mod_gap` helper, exact on 32-bit targets too. Tests: `zenith-float-num/tests/exponent_range.rs::rem_extreme_spread_odd_divisor_is_exact`, `rem_boundary_cases`, `rem_small_precision_exhaustive_matches_u128_oracle`.

- **Fix (2.0): `Mantissa::pow_mod` no longer drops the top bit of the exponent.** Its find-first-bit loop shifted before checking, so an exponent at or above `2^(WORD_BIT_SIZE - 1)` lost its top bit (`2^(2^63 + 61)` came back as `2^61`). The top bit is now checked before shifting. Covered by `num::tests::pow2_mod_gap_matches_pow_mod_and_u128_oracle`, which checks both implementations against a u128 oracle at gaps up to `i128::MAX`.

- **Tests (2.0): `rem` at extreme exponent spreads** (Vera). The odd-divisor test checks the remainder of the largest finite number by 7 at the bottom of the range against an independent u128 oracle, so a saturated or wrapped gap gives a different remainder, not just a panic; the boundary cases pin equal operands, dividend below divisor, negative divisor, and smallest normal by smallest subnormal; the exhaustive grid checks every mantissa pair at precisions 1..=5 against the oracle.

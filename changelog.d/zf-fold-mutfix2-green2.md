- zenith-float-num: bound the loops behind the num-slice OOM and timeouts. `ExactInt::from_exact_num` takes exactly `ceil(e / 64)` limbs for `|x| < 2^e` and returns `None` if the quotient is not zero after them (before, a broken `ldexp` gave a divisor of 0 and the limb vector grew until the 32 GB OOM kill). `random_unit_open` redraws a zero at most 64 times, then returns NaN (`PrecisionRetryExhausted`). The real Γ/ψ shift loops (`shift_up`) and the complex gamma family (`shift_while_small`) return `PrecisionRetryExhausted` right away when `2^(e_min-1) - |z|` (the fewest steps that can reach the target) is above the cap, rather than running up to 2^20 full-width steps. The complex shift cap now stops at 2^20, the real kernel's `STIRLING_SHIFT_MAX` (before, it was 2^(k-1)+2 with no limit). The complex 2F1 series stores its per-term `n + a`, `n + b` and `(n + c)(n + 1)` without all-zero low words, and runs its stop test on the leading 128 bits of the term and sum. Values are the same, but at 16640 bits a series pass costs 9.5 s instead of 88 s (debug). Correct inputs give the same results; the full `zenith-float-num` suite passes.

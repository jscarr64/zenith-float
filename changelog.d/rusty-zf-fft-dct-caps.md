- FFT / DSP caps (zenith-float 2.0 Slice 2): `FFT_MAX_POINTS` raised 4,096 → 65,536 and now public (`zenith_float_num::FFT_MAX_POINTS`); `DSP_MAX_POINTS` raised 2,048 → 16,384. New per-call caps: `ExactNumArray::fft_with_max_points` / `ifft_with_max_points(&self, max_points, p, rm, cc)` and `dct_with_max_points`, `idct_with_max_points`, `dst_with_max_points`, `idst_with_max_points`, `fft_real_with_max_points`, `ifft_real_with_max_points(signal, max_points, p, rm, cc)`; the old entry points call them with the defaults. `n > max_points` (or `max_points = 0`) is `None` before any copy or allocation. New `check_alloc_bytes(count) -> Option<()>` refuses working buffers whose byte size overflows or exceeds `isize::MAX`; FFT/DSP buffers are reserved with `try_reserve_exact` after it, so a huge per-call cap fails with `None`, not a panic. The DSP transforms' internal `2N`-point FFT is sized by the DSP length (not capped by `FFT_MAX_POINTS`), so a DSP cap above 32,768 works. `idct` / `idst` are now `O(N log N)` through one `2N`-point FFT (were `O(N²)` direct sums with a trig call per term, infeasible at N = 16,384); results agree with the defining sums. The FFT now runs its butterflies at `p + 2·log2(n) + 16` bits and rounds each output once to `p` with `rm` (before, the twiddle recurrence at `p` lost up to ~log2(n) bits, about 16 at the new default); `p = 0` stays refused. Tested in `ieee_soft/array.rs` and `dsp.rs` unit tests (no new target): caps at the defaults and per call, n = 8,192 cosine-bin / ifft / Parseval at p = 128, n = 65,536 and 131,072 impulse at p = 64, N = 4,096 DCT orthogonality and round trips, N = 16,384 and 32,768 transforms, direct-sum agreement of the fast inverses.

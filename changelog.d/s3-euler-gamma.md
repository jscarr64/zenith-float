- S3: Euler–Mascheroni γ is now computed by Brent–McMillan B1 with exact integer binary splitting (`N = 5n` terms, `n = 2^j`, truncation below `2^−(p+46)`, one final division), about 90× faster at p = 65536 (0.49 s release, cold, was 44 s; 1.4 s at p = 131072). `Consts::euler_gamma(p, rm)` now rounds at exactly `p` bits like `Consts::pi` (it rounded to whole words); `RoundingMode::None` callers are unchanged. No new public API.
